# W0 — Landing page at lookly.uz

Started at `4c333c8` (2026-11-28). Owner request of 2026-11-28; mockups
`apps/web/design/landing-desktop.png` and `landing-mobile.png`; ADR 0012 (mobile app
only, the website is a landing page).

## Problem

lookly.uz has nothing to show a visitor: the web app answers a guest with its sign-in
page. Users will use Lookly through the mobile app only, so the site's job is to say
what Lookly does and send visitors to the stores.

## Solution

A static, public page in `apps/web` built to the approved mockup, with the app's
palette and the Manrope font: header with section links and a download button; hero
(title, line, paragraph, store badges, a short trust row, the phone illustration);
four features; three steps; privacy; a dark download banner; questions and answers;
footer with the legal pages.

- **Address.** `/landing` until B7, then `/` (the user web pages the root serves today
  are retired in B7, ADR 0012; the browser suite still drives them until then).
- **Store badges.** The official Google Play badge in Uzbek ("Google Play orqali
  oling") and the official App Store badge (Apple's site gave the English one). Without
  a store link a badge is shown dimmed, not as a link, with "Tez kunda" under it. The
  links are set in the admin panel (B8/W2); until then none is set.
- **Left out of the mockup, by decision:** "Brauzerda ochish" (no web version); the QR
  code (it would point to a store, and there is no store link yet); the Telegram and
  "Aloqa" links (the owner gives the addresses).
- **Texts** live in `messages/uz.json` (`landing`), so the page can follow the app into
  English and Russian later (B10/M3).
- **Illustrations** are cut out of the owner's sheet (a drawn checkerboard, not real
  transparency) into transparent WebP files under `public/landing/`; about 450 px
  each, so a sharper hero picture is asked of the owner for large screens.

## User stories

1. A visitor on a phone or a computer sees what Lookly does, how it works and how
   their data is treated, in Uzbek, without signing in.
2. A visitor finds the store badges in the hero and the banner; while the app is not
   in a store, the badge says "Tez kunda" and is not a link.
3. A visitor reads the privacy policy and the terms from the footer.
4. A visitor using a keyboard or a screen reader can reach every section, link and
   answer (headings in order, the answers as native disclosure widgets).

## Out of scope

The page's settings in the admin panel (B8/W2); the account deletion request page
(needs the owner's contact address; before the store submission, M7); other
languages (B10/M3); moving to `/` (B7).

## Testing

- e2e (`e2e/landing.spec.ts`, both modes): the page opens signed out; every section
  heading; the badges in their "Tez kunda" state and no store link; no "Brauzerda
  ochish"; the legal links open; the answers open and close; at 360 px there is no
  sideways scrolling.
- Unit: the store badge renders a link when a link is given and the "Tez kunda" state
  when none is.
